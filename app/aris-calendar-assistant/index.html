Ari's Calendar Assistant

Ari's Calendar Assistant is a personal tool that adds events to Google Calendar. You send it an event, such as a concert ticket or a short message with a title, time and place, and it creates the event on your calendar. It can also delete an event it created when you ask.

It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner and the people they invite. It is not sold, and it shows no ads.

Google data it uses

With your permission it reads your calendar list and creates, updates and deletes events on your Google Calendar. It does nothing else with your Google account.
