Privacy policy

Ari's Calendar Assistant · Effective 10 November 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant access, the app uses the Google Calendar API to read your list of calendars and to create, update and delete events on them. It accesses calendar data only to carry out a request you make, such as "add this concert to my calendar".

How the data is used

  • Event details you send (title, time, location, description) are written to your Google Calendar.
  • The app does not sell, rent or share your Google data with anyone.
  • The app does not use your Google data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your calendar data unless you ask for help with a problem, or the law requires it.

Storage

The OAuth token Google issues is stored only on the computer that runs the app. The app keeps a local log of the requests it handled, used only to fix errors. Nothing is stored on a third-party server other than Google's own.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the local token and logs deleted, contact the developer below.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
